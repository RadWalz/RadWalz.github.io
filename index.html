Riu

Private automation tool of Darius Lozano

Riu is a personal automation assistant that runs on Darius Lozano's own computer. It helps him move his own video and work files to and from his own Google Drive. It is not offered to the public and has no other users.

Contact: darenzlozano@gmail.com

Privacy Policy

Last updated: October 16, 2026

Riu accesses Google user data (Google Drive files and, when asked, Google Sheets and Docs) only for the single account owner, Darius Lozano, and only to carry out tasks he starts himself, such as uploading or downloading his own files.

  • Data stays on the owner's own computer and in his own Google account.
  • No Google user data is sold, shared with, or transferred to any third party.
  • No data is used for advertising or for training AI models.
  • Sign-in tokens are stored encrypted on the owner's computer and can be removed at any time at myaccount.google.com, Security, Third-party apps.

Riu's use of information received from Google APIs adheres to the Google API Services User Data Policy, including the Limited Use requirements.

Terms

Riu is a private tool for its owner only. There are no other users and no service is offered.
